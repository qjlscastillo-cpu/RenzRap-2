📜 Our History

Founded in 2007 by Mrs. Felicitas Masi, Renz Rap Auto Supply has served Quezon City for nearly two decades. After 18 years of manual record-keeping, the business is now transitioning to a digitalized workflow to better manage its expanding inventory and customer base.

🔬 Project Scope

This system is a LAN-based Product Management Solution designed to automate core business operations:

Real-time tracking of 1,000+ automotive products.
Automated Point-of-Sale (POS) and sales recording.
Generation of Daily, Weekly, and Monthly reports.
Secure local database management using SQLite.
